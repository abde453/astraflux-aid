AI & PINN research module Planned / not yet implemented

No machine-learning or PINN model is connected. Nothing in this platform is "AI-predicted". Requirements before activation:

  • Training-data scope: sources, number of formulations, conditions, licensing
  • Validation metrics on unseen formulations (RMSE, f2, calibration curves)
  • Uncertainty estimates (ensembles or Bayesian) and applicability-domain checks
  • Physics constraints for PINNs: governing equations, boundary/initial conditions, loss weights
  • Model card: version, training date, limitations
